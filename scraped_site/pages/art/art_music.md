Vetrisaipadal

Explore a special project born from my deep desire to help our community lift their voices in praise. By embracing modern AI technology, I separate vocals from traditional Tamil Catholic songs to create refined, high-quality backing tracks. What began as a humble initiative has blossomed into an ongoing YouTube library of over 500 Karaoke Tracks, with new melodies added every week. While my heart as a friar reminds us that these tracks are not meant for the Liturgy of the Holy Mass, they hold a beautiful purpose elsewhere: to inspire singers, learners, and choirs to choose sacred devotional music over cinema songs for their cultural and public events. 'Let us sing along with music; let us glorify God.'
