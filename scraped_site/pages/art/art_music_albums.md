#Read the Historical Note 

Every journey has a humble beginning. Vizhigalile holds a very special place in my heart as my debut musical album, born in 2010 during my early days as a Capuchin. Driven purely by grace and divine inspiration rather than formal classical training, these hymns first took shape in the seminary. Later, during my summer holidays, we breathed new life into these 15 songs with the heartfelt voices of the Malayadipatty parish choir and a simple keyboard. Released on the joyous occasion of the 125th anniversary of the St. Francis Xavier church renovation, this album was recorded with minimal resources but boundless passion—giving me the foundational confidence to walk this beautiful musical path.

#Read the Historical Note 

Every sacred milestone has its hidden season of quietude. Anbanay holds a deeply reverent place in my memory as an archival testament to my novitiate—a profound time entirely set apart for prayer, silence, and interior reflection. Driven by an unexpected wave of inner lyrical and musical inspiration rather than formal classical training, a significant body of sacred compositions, numbering well over thirty, took form in the quiet of the seminary.

This album is a curated collection representing just one set of these songs. For this specific collection, original Franciscan hymns were lovingly translated into Tamil and given a structured lyrical form. This period also marked the very initiation of my journey into Classical Carnatic music, where various traditional ragas were mindfully explored and woven into these particular pieces.

Later, these hymns were taught to a grand choir of 60 formees—both brothers and sisters—filling the Holy Week services with collective prayer. To preserve the raw, prayerful energy of that moment, the entire album was recorded without a single line of digital programming, captured using only a basic keyboard and the unfiltered voices of the brothers. Officially released on the joyous milestone of my First Profession on May 9, 2013, this CD archive stands as one beautifully preserved chapter, remaining a foundational pillar that gave me the baseline confidence to walk this musical path.
