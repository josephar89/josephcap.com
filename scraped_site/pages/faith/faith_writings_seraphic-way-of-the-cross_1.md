English

The year 2026 marks the 800th anniversary of the Transitus, the passing of St. Francis of Assisi into eternal glory. This Seraphic Way of the Cross invites pilgrims into a "spirituality of conformation," correlating the fourteen stations of Christ's Passion with pivotal moments in the life of the Alter Christus. By walking this path, we seek to transform our own bitterness into "sweetness of soul and body" and rediscover the Franciscan legacies of mercy, fraternal life, and peace.
